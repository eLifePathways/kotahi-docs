Kotahi's previous documentation lived at `kotahi.community`, a domain that has
since expired. Most of the pages here were recovered from an archive of that
site taken on **13 July 2026** and converted automatically. They have **not**

Every page carries a status banner saying whether it has been verified,
converted and awaiting review, or newly written. Please read those banners —
